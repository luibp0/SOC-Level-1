# SOC Role in Blue Team

## Overview

This room introduces the role of a Security Operations Center (SOC) within the Blue Team. It explains how SOC teams monitor, detect, investigate, and respond to cybersecurity threats and incidents.

## Topics Covered

- Security Operations Center (SOC)
- SOC Roles and Responsibilities
- Security Monitoring
- Threat Detection
- Alert Triage
- Incident Investigation
- Incident Response
- Threat Intelligence
- SOC Escalation Process

## SOC Analyst Levels

- **Level 1 (L1):** Monitor and triage security alerts
- **Level 2 (L2):** Perform deeper investigation and incident analysis
- **Level 3 (L3):** Handle advanced threats and complex incidents

## Alert Triage

SOC analysts investigate alerts by answering important questions such as:

- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- Where did it happen?
- Who was involved?
- Why did it happen?

## Skills Learned

- Understanding how a SOC operates
- Identifying the responsibilities of SOC analysts
- Performing basic alert triage
- Understanding the escalation process
- Understanding incident detection and response
- Using threat intelligence during investigations

## Key Takeaway

The SOC is a central part of the Blue Team, responsible for continuously monitoring the environment, detecting suspicious activity, investigating security alerts, and responding to potential threats.
