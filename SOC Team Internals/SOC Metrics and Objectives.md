# SOC Metrics and Objectives

## Overview

This room focuses on the metrics and objectives used to measure the performance and effectiveness of a Security Operations Center (SOC).
It explains how SOC teams track alerts, response times, detection effectiveness, and service targets.

## Common SOC Metrics

SOC teams use different metrics to evaluate their performance:

- **Alerts Count:** Number of security alerts handled by analysts.
- **False Positive Rate:** Percentage of alerts incorrectly identified as threats.
- **Alert Escalation Rate:** Percentage of alerts escalated for further investigation.
- **Threat Detection Rate:** Ability of the SOC to successfully detect real threats.

## Important SOC Time Metrics

- **MTTD (Mean Time to Detect):** Time required to detect a security threat.
- **MTTA (Mean Time to Acknowledge):** Time required for an analyst to acknowledge an alert.
- **MTTR (Mean Time to Respond):** Time required to respond to and handle an incident.

## Service Level Agreements

A Service Level Agreement (SLA) defines expected performance targets for SOC operations.

Examples include:

- 24/7 SOC availability
- Target detection times
- Target acknowledgement times
- Target response times
- Incident escalation requirements

## Why SOC Metrics Are Important

SOC metrics help organizations:

- Measure SOC performance
- Identify areas for improvement
- Reduce response times
- Evaluate detection effectiveness
- Monitor analyst workload
- Maintain security service standards

## Key Takeaway

SOC metrics and objectives help measure how efficiently a security team detects, investigates, and responds to threats. Tracking metrics such as MTTD, MTTA, and MTTR helps improve overall SOC performance.
