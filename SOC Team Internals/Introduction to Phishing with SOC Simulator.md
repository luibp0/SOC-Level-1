## Phishing Investigation Process

When investigating a phishing alert, a SOC analyst should review:

- **Sender:** Check the sender's email address and domain.
- **Recipient:** Identify the targeted user.
- **Subject:** Examine the email subject for suspicious or urgent language.
- **Links:** Analyze URLs and domains included in the email.
- **Attachments:** Check for potentially malicious files.
- **Network Activity:** Look for connections related to suspicious links or domains.
- **Security Logs:** Correlate email activity with firewall or other security events.

## Common Phishing Indicators

Some common signs of phishing include:

- Suspicious or misspelled domains
- Fake login pages
- URL shorteners
- Unexpected links or attachments
- Impersonation of trusted companies
- Urgent or threatening messages
- Requests for credentials or sensitive information
